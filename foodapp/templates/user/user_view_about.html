🍽️ About RecipeHub

A community-driven platform where food lovers share, discover and connect through recipes.

What is RecipeHub?

RecipeHub is an interactive recipe sharing platform that allows users and administrators to create, explore, like, and comment on delicious recipes.

Whether you're a home cook or a food enthusiast, RecipeHub makes discovering and sharing recipes simple, engaging, and fun.

✨ Key Features

👩‍🍳 User Recipes

Users can upload their own recipes with images, videos, ingredients, and step-by-step instructions.

❤️ Likes & Comments

Engage with recipes by liking them and sharing your thoughts through comments.

🔍 Smart Search

Easily search recipes by name or filter them by categories to find exactly what you're craving.

🛠️ Admin Management

Administrators can add official recipes, manage categories, and monitor user interactions such as likes and comments.

This ensures quality control and smooth platform management.

🎯 Our Vision

To build a vibrant cooking community where recipes are not just shared — they are experienced, appreciated, and celebrated.
